# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We commit to a harassment-free, accessible and constructive community regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, religion, socioeconomic status or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not pressure anyone to disclose personal data.
- Use inclusive language and make room for less experienced contributors.
- Treat accessibility, safety and user agency as product requirements.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, discrimination, deliberate misinformation, publishing private reports, or using project spaces to profile people without consent are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact or security-reporting channel. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently remove participants. Reports will be handled with minimum necessary disclosure and without retaliation.

This policy applies to repository spaces, project events and public representation of the project.
