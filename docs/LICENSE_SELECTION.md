# License decision record

The repository already used the MIT license, so the open-source cleanup retains MIT and corrects the template copyright to “ZhixingOS contributors”. MIT is simple and permissive, which lowers adoption friction but permits proprietary forks and hosted services without source publication.

If the community later wants reciprocal obligations, changing to Apache-2.0 or AGPL-3.0 requires confirming copyright ownership or contributor consent, auditing all third-party assets, updating package metadata, and documenting the transition boundary. A license change must follow `GOVERNANCE.md`; do not replace `LICENSE` casually.

Third-party assets are not automatically relicensed by the repository license. Keep their notices and verify redistribution terms before every release.
